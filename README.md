# Optimizing-Textile-Production-Efficiency
## Background:
"TexFab Pvt. Ltd." is a textile manufacturing company based in Tiruppur, India, producing cotton and polyester fabric for domestic and export markets. The company operates 24/7 with three shifts and has been facing challenges in maintaining production efficiency and meeting delivery deadlines.

## Problem Statement:
Despite having modern machinery, TexFab Pvt. Ltd. experiences fluctuating production output, high defect rates, and frequent machine downtimes. Management wants to analyze production KPIs and identify the factors impacting efficiency and quality.

## Objectives:
- Track and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) in real-time.
- Identify correlations between machine downtimes, defect rates, and productivity.
- Recommend process improvements using insights from the dataset.

## Insights Expected:
- Machines with consistently low efficiency or high defect rates.
- Shifts with higher average downtime.
- Impact of temperature and humidity on defect rates.
- Operator performance trends.
